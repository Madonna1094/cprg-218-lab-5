The LuxeLocks Story

Where it all began...

Founded in 2018 by master stylist Sarah Chen, LuxeLocks Hair Studio was born from a simple dream: to create a space where artistry meets luxury, and every client leaves feeling their absolute best.

After 15 years of experience in high-end salons across Toronto and Vancouver, Sarah brought her vision to Calgary, combining cutting-edge techniques with personalized service that treats each client as a unique canvas.

Today, our team of award-winning stylists continues this tradition, staying at the forefront of hair fashion while maintaining the warm, welcoming atmosphere that makes LuxeLocks feel like home.
